# B2W + JasonAI Brand Identity

Status: adopted for the website rebuild

## The decision

B2W is a product company building practical AI exclusively for General Contracting businesses. Its first product makes project communication and job knowledge easier to find, review, and use.

JasonAI is the first active product inside B2W. It is an AI assistant for general contractors, delivered through WhatsApp after a founder-led integration. It does not present itself as a separate company or a complete project-management platform.

The identity is built around one idea:

> Turn business noise into work that moves.

## Brand architecture

### B2W — the parent

- Role: accountable product company and product builder.
- Promise: make AI usable by connecting better decisions, practical systems, and accountable implementation.
- Commercial layers: JasonAI subscription, guided WhatsApp setup, product workflows, and Resources.
- Character: calm, exact, operational, ambitious without pretending the future is already available.

### JasonAI — the current product

- Role: administrative AI assistant.
- Current customer: owner-led general contracting businesses.
- Current job: search approved business communication and summarize job context through WhatsApp.
- Current price: $99 per month.
- Integration: one-time $2,000 WhatsApp setup, approved-source mapping, onboarding, and first-workflow validation.
- Product signal: mineral blue within the B2W parent system.

### Clara — the next interaction direction

- Role today: a source of interaction design principles, not a launched public product.
- Reusable principle: Capture → Structure → Review → Use.
- Future direction: management-agent workflows only after JasonAI repeatability is proven.

### Gurge / connected system — the potential future

- Role today: internal tracking and longer-term operator-agent direction.
- Future direction: governed connected agents, performance tracking, risk controls, reporting, and insights.
- Public rule: do not use the future platform to justify current product claims or price.

## Voice

B2W should sound like a founder who understands the business and is willing to be specific.

Use:

- direct statements;
- concrete business conditions;
- short declarative headlines;
- visible boundaries;
- plain-language explanations of AI;
- one clear next action.

Avoid:

- generic “transformation” language;
- anthropomorphic claims that imply independent authority;
- platform claims before the platform exists;
- technical language without a business consequence;
- urgency manufactured through discounts or countdown language;
- several equally weighted calls to action.

## Visual identity

### Parent palette

- Paper — `#F3F0E8`: primary B2W background.
- Deep paper — `#E5E0D5`: section contrast and operating diagrams.
- Ink — `#111315`: typography, high-authority surfaces, and the primary parent color.
- White — `#FFFFFF`: documents, review surfaces, and clear content fields.

### Product and semantic colors

- JasonAI mineral blue — `#315F79`.
- JasonAI deep blue — `#173D52`.
- JasonAI light field — `#DCE9EE`.
- Verified green — `#39745F`.
- Planning plum — `#6D5C79`.
- Evidence brass — `#C5A36A`.
- Risk red — `#B94235`.

Color is functional. B2W remains predominantly ink and paper. Mineral blue identifies JasonAI interactions, brass marks evidence or sequence, green marks verified/current states, plum marks planning, and red marks risk.

### Typography

- Family: Inter.
- Display: very large, medium-weight, tightly tracked, and written as an operating statement.
- Body: regular weight with generous leading.
- Metadata: small uppercase or monospaced labels used for sequence, status, and evidence.

The typography keeps the strongest quality of the previous live site—editorial scale—while removing the feeling that each route belongs to a different company.

### Mark

The animated triangular Clara-derived mark remains the B2W parent mark. It is paired with the B2W wordmark where identity must be explicit and may reveal the wordmark in compact navigation. JasonAI may add a mineral-blue signal treatment but does not receive a separate parent logo.

## Layout system

- Maximum public content width: 1440px.
- Primary navigation height: 80px.
- Section rhythm: 64–96px vertical padding.
- Content is organized with borders, fields, and sequence rather than stacked decorative cards.
- Large statements are paired with smaller operational explanations.
- Dark sections establish authority; light document-like surfaces support review.
- Rounded pills are reserved for controls, status, and actions. Primary information architecture uses straighter containers.

## Motion system

Motion must explain a state change.

- Entrance: blur-and-rise for the first meaningful reveal.
- Identity: a short descramble resolves the B2W product-company label on entry.
- Interaction: primary navigation and CTA labels descramble on hover or keyboard focus.
- In-view: restrained opacity and vertical movement.
- Signal: slow horizontal movement across a line or field to show information being processed.
- Transformation: content changes through Capture, Structure, Review, and Use states.
- Hover: small directional movement that confirms a link.
- Reduced motion: all nonessential animation resolves immediately.

The website does not use motion as decoration or hide essential content inside scroll choreography.

## Website hierarchy

### Company orientation

The homepage states what B2W is, what can be bought now, how the service layers connect, and how the product can grow without presenting roadmap work as current.

### Information architecture

The homepage explains JasonAI through the product sequence Ask → Find → Summarize. Guided setup and founder consultation support adoption of the product without becoming separate service-company offers.

### User experience

JasonAI begins with the owner’s problem, demonstrates one WhatsApp question moving through search and summary, makes review visible, and then explains price and setup.

### Interaction design

Resources and product demonstrations reuse the Clara sequence: Capture → Structure → Review → Use. The user can advance the state, inspect the transformation, and see a clear completion condition.

## What was retained from the live site

- the Clara-derived animated B2W mark;
- oversized, editorial typography;
- warm off-white surfaces;
- high-contrast black sections;
- blur-and-rise entrance motion;
- active product demonstrations;
- the direct founder-led tone of the strongest JasonAI copy;
- product-specific accent color inside a shared company system.

## What changed

- the site no longer behaves like separate B2W, JasonAI, and Clara websites;
- generic consulting categories are replaced by Strategy, Systems, and Implementation;
- the current launch offer is explicit: $99/month, $2,000 WhatsApp setup, and founder consultation for contracting workflows;
- discount-led pre-launch pricing is removed from the primary commercial architecture;
- the parent palette is simplified from many competing brand colors to ink, paper, and one active JasonAI signal;
- rounded SaaS-card styling is reduced in favor of fields, rules, and document-like operating structure;
- current, in-development, and future capability remain visibly distinct.

## Governance

Before publishing a new claim, page, product, or workflow:

1. identify whether it belongs to B2W, JasonAI, Clara direction, or the future connected system;
2. label the capability Available now, In development, Planned, or Future;
3. name the business condition and the responsible human reviewer;
4. connect the claim to a current commercial offer or leave it as direction;
5. use the shared navigation, typography, color, status, and motion system;
6. end the page with one primary next action.
