Proposal for Business Sale Preparation and Opportunity Packaging


Objective
Prepare the business for sale by organizing financial, operational, and market information into a clear, professional package that communicates the opportunity to potential buyers. This process helps increase buyer confidence, improve perceived value, and streamline the sale process.
_____________________________________________________________________________________________
1. Business Opportunity Overview Webpage
Develop a professional digital overview that presents the business in a clear and compelling format for prospective buyers.


Includes:
* Business overview and history
* Description of products or services
* Customer profile and demand drivers
* Financial highlights and revenue model
* Competitive positioning and market overview
* Key assets included in the sale
* Growth opportunities for a new owner


Deliverable
A structured Business Opportunity Page designed to clearly communicate the value of the business.


2. Valuation Modeling & Estimated Sale Range
Develop an estimated valuation range based on available financial and operational data.


Includes:
* Review of historical revenue and profitability
* Normalized owner earnings analysis
* Benchmarking against comparable business sales
* Estimated valuation range based on market multiples


Deliverable
A valuation model and summary explaining potential sale price ranges.


3. Operations Documentation & SOP Packaging
Organize and formalize the operational knowledge of the business so it can transfer smoothly to a buyer.


Includes:
* Documentation of key processes and workflows
* Creation or formalization of Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)
* Vendor and supplier documentation
* Marketing and customer acquisition processes
* Staff roles and operational responsibilities


Deliverable
A structured Business Operations Manual to help a new owner quickly understand and operate the business.


4. Buyer Due Diligence Package
Prepare and organize the materials commonly requested by prospective buyers so the business can be clearly evaluated.


Includes:
* Business summary and key investment highlights
* Organized financial summaries
* Vendor and supplier overview
* Lease or location information
* Equipment and asset inventory
* Marketing channels and digital assets
* Growth opportunities and common buyer questions


Deliverable
A structured Buyer Information Package / Data Room that the owner can share with qualified buyers.




Summary of Deliverables
At the end of the engagement, the business will have:


1. A professional Business Opportunity Overview Webpage
2. A valuation estimate and financial analysis
3. A documented Business Operations Manual supporting transferable operations
4. A structured Buyer Information Package / Data Room containing financial summaries, operational documentation, and key business information for prospective buyers
5. A clear narrative of the business opportunity and growth potential for prospective buyers




Timeline
Typical engagement timeline: 3 weeks
* Week 1 - Discovery and financial review
* Week 2 - Market research and valuation modeling
* Week 3 - Operational documentation and SOP packaging/ Sales materials and final deliverables

level of research required


Role Clarification
B2W provides business preparation, research, and documentation services only. B2W does not act as a business broker or intermediary and does not solicit buyers or negotiate sale terms. All communications with prospective buyers and any sale negotiations will be conducted directly by the business owner or their licensed broker.

Key Terms and Assumptions


1. Scope of Work
Services are limited to the preparation, analysis, and documentation outlined in the selected option(s) above. B2W provides advisory and preparation services only.


2. Information Provided by Client
The client is responsible for providing accurate financial, operational, and business information required to perform the services. B2W will rely on the information provided and does not independently audit or verify its accuracy.


3. Financial Analysis and Valuation
Any valuation estimates or financial analyses are illustrative and based on available data and market benchmarks. They are not formal appraisals and do not guarantee a specific sale price or transaction outcome.


4. Market Research
Market research and competitive analysis are based on publicly available information and industry data available at the time of analysis. Market conditions may change over time and may affect the accuracy or relevance of findings.


5. Documentation and SOP Development
Operational documentation and SOPs will be developed based on interviews, available records, and observations of current processes. These materials are intended as guidance and may require future updates as the business evolves.


6. Client Participation
Timely client participation, including interviews, document sharing, and operational clarification, is required to complete the engagement within the estimated timeline.


7. Buyer Communication and Brokerage Activities
B2W does not act as a licensed business broker, intermediary, or advisor in negotiating a transaction. B2W will not solicit buyers, negotiate sale terms, or participate in brokerage activities. All communications with prospective buyers and any transaction negotiations will be conducted by the business owner or their licensed broker.


8. Use of Materials
All materials prepared as part of the engagement are intended for use by the client in presenting the business opportunity to prospective buyers. The client retains ownership of materials produced specifically for their business. B2W reserves the right to reference non-confidential aspects of the engagement for portfolio or case study purposes.


9. Confidentiality
Both parties agree to maintain confidentiality of non-public financial, operational, and strategic information shared during the engagement.


10. Timeline and Deliverables
Project timelines are estimates based on timely client participation and information availability. Delays in receiving required information may extend the project timeline.


11. Payment Terms
Payment terms will be outlined in the selected package. Unless otherwise agreed, payment is due at the start of the engagement.


12. No Legal, Tax, or Investment Advice
B2W does not provide legal, tax, accounting, or investment advice. Clients should consult qualified professionals for advice related to legal matters, taxation, accounting treatment, or transaction structuring.


13. No Guarantee of Sale
While the services are designed to improve the presentation and preparedness of the business for sale, B2W does not guarantee that a sale will occur or that any specific valuation or transaction outcome will be achieved.
