Document 2: The B2W Health Check Report (Format)
Goal: The standard presentation delivered to the owner.

I. Executive Summary
*   The "One-Liner": The business's current status in 15 words.
*   The Scorecard:
    *   Marketing Score: 0-100 (Based on digital presence/conversion)
    *   Operations Score: 0-100 (Based on efficiency/service)
    *   Financial Health Score: 0-100 (Based on revenue estimation/overhead)

II. Deep Dives
*   Marketing Deep Dive: Summary of current assets vs. missed opportunities.
*   Operations Deep Dive: Bottlenecks identified (e.g., slow service, inventory issues).
*   Financial Deep Dive: Key areas of revenue leakage or cost inefficiencies.

III. The "Internal Data" Layer
*   (This section is only populated if the owner grants access to POS/Analytics).
*   Data Correlation: Connecting public review sentiment (e.g., "Food is cold") to internal data (e.g., POS timestamps showing long prep-to-table lag).

IV. B2W Roadmap
*   Immediate Wins: Low-hanging fruit to fix in 30 days.
*   Strategic Initiatives: 90-day plan.
