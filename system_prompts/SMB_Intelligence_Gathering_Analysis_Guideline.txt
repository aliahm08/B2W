Document 1: SMB Intelligence Gathering & Analysis Guideline
Goal: Assess a business using only public data to identify "pain points" before the first meeting.

1. Marketing Intelligence (The Face)
*   Presence Audit: Do they have active social media? (Check Instagram post frequency, TikTok trends).
*   Reputation Management: Analyze Google Maps/Yelp. Look for specific complaints about service or food consistency. Identify the ratio of positive to negative reviews.
*   Digital Experience: Is their website functional? Is it mobile-friendly? Do they have online ordering? (If not, that's an immediate pain point).
*   Publicity/Buzz: Search local blogs, news outlets, and "best of" lists. If they aren't being mentioned, their brand equity is low.

2. Operations Intelligence (The Engine)
*   Location Analysis: Use Google Maps traffic/foot traffic density.
*   Menu/Offering Strategy: How complex is the menu? (High complexity = high food waste). Are prices competitive compared to the neighborhood?
*   Customer Sentiment on Ops: Search reviews for keywords like "slow service," "long wait times," "confusing layout," or "rude staff."

3. Financial Estimation (The Bottom Line)
*   Volume Proxy: Use review frequency on Google/Yelp to estimate monthly customer traffic.
*   AOV (Average Order Value) Estimation: Build a menu price average to estimate the ticket size.
*   Back-of-Napkin Revenue: Daily Volume x AOV x 30 days = Estimated Monthly Revenue.
*   Overhead Inference: Estimate rent based on location (Zip code/commercial rates) and staff size based on service intensity.
